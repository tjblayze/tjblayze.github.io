Game Project - Rosehaven Studios

The Stitch Master: Awakening

Role: Creator & game designer - character, level & UI design

Overview

Rosehaven Studios' flagship - in development with the NeoStack team. Above: Stitches in profile before the glowing Dark Shard. Character, level design, and UI detail follow below - this is the one Stitch Master entry on the portfolio.

Role & Tools

Timothy Rose is the creator and designer behind The Stitch Master: Awakening at Rosehaven Studios - leading character design and development, level design, game design, and UI design, in development with the NeoStack team.

  • Unreal Engine 5.6
  • Blender
  • Substance 3D Painter

Character Design & Development

Front view first: Stitches holding the Ember Shard in-game, then the build itself - Blender gray views, the face close-up with the modeled thread and button detail, a three-quarter view pulled from the turntable, the textured model in a graybox test level, and the full turntable video.

Turntable - 16-second orbit of the model in Blender. Playable video, not a GIF.

Stitches - modeled by Timothy Rose in Blender, textured in Substance Painter.

The supporting cast - William and the Hollowed - were created differently: William - AI-generated under my art direction The Hollowed - AI-generated under my art direction They appear in the level shots below.

Level Design

Levels designed and built by Timothy Rose - layout, placement, pacing, and storytelling - even where individual props came from asset packs. The workshop's oversized hand plane, the fireplace beats, the Spirit Gate, and the Hollowed encounter are all placed to guide the player through the space.

Game Design

The design is built around Stitches' journey through hand-placed spaces: the Ember Shard and Dark Shard as the story's central objects, giant candle checkpoints marking progress, the Spirit Gate portal connecting the world, and the Hollowed as the encounter that tests the player. Levels carry the storytelling - the workshop, the fireplace beats, and the gate are placed to guide pacing, not just to fill space - and the item and prompt UI below keeps the player oriented without breaking the mood.

UI Design

Item cards designed by Timothy Rose, plus the white-on-transparent keyboard prompt icons - shown here on dark chips so the white artwork reads clearly, the way it appears in-game.
