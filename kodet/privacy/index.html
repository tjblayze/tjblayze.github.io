Kodet Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Who we are

Kodet.AI ("Kodet") is a channel assistant for YouTube creators, made by Rootline Industries, LLC. This policy explains what Kodet collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions go to support@timothyjrose.com.

What Kodet collects

  • Your account: your name and the email you sign up with.
  • Accounts you connect: when you connect YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or Discord, Kodet keeps the access the platform grants (a sign-in token), your channel, account or page name and ID, and the numbers that platform shares, such as views, watch time, subscribers and comments.
  • What you post through Kodet: the videos, titles, captions and pictures you ask Kodet to post, and the links that come back.
  • What you add yourself: goals, expenses and notes you type or upload.

How Kodet uses it

  • To show your channel's numbers, goals and milestones back to you.
  • To post your videos where you tell it to, at the time you pick. Kodet never posts without your approval.
  • To write suggestions, reports and reminders for you.

Kodet does not sell your information, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone except the platforms you connect, and only to do what you asked.

Connected platforms

Kodet uses each platform's official API. Your use of those platforms is also covered by their own terms and privacy policies, including the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Kodet's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How long Kodet keeps it

Kodet keeps your information while your account is open. If you disconnect a platform, Kodet stops reading from it at once and erases that platform's data after 90 days, so you can reconnect without losing your history. You can ask us to erase it sooner.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any platform inside Kodet, or remove Kodet's access from the platform itself (for Google, at myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • Email support@timothyjrose.com to see, correct or delete your information. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Sign-in tokens and your data are stored on Kodet's own server and are never shown in the app or sent to anyone else. No system is perfect, and we will tell you promptly if your information is ever exposed.

Children

Kodet is not meant for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and big changes are announced inside Kodet first.
