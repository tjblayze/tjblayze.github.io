# Kodet launch ad: brief and script

Draft, Nov 19. For Timothy to approve. Nothing gets posted without his OK.

## The one question the ad answers

**Why would a creator use Kodet?**

YouTube Studio tells you *what* happened: 61% of viewers swiped away. It never tells you *why*, or what to do about it. Kodet does. It reads your numbers, compares the video to your own best ones, says in plain English what went wrong, and plans the next post with you.

**Line to build the ad around:**
> YouTube tells you what happened. Kodet tells you why.

## Who it's for

Small and mid-size creators who post Shorts, Reels and TikToks, have no analyst or editor, and keep guessing why one video hits and the next one dies.

## What the ad must not claim

Show only features Kodet has today. Use real screen recordings of Kodet Insights and real numbers from Timothy's channel, not mock-ups. The proof below comes from the Nov 11 post-mortem on the board.

## Script: 30 seconds, vertical 9:16

The ad follows Kodet's own advice. It opens on the payoff at full brightness, has no slow intro, and doesn't end on black.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Full brightness. A YouTube Studio retention graph falling off a cliff. A big red **61% swiped away** stamps on. | "61% of people swiped away my Short." |
| 0:02–0:05 | Studio's numbers fill the screen as a wall of data, then blur. | "YouTube told me *what* happened. Not *why*." |
| 0:05–0:08 | Kodet logo pops in. The Kodet Insights app slides up on a phone. | "So I asked Kodet." |
| 0:08–0:16 | The Short plays in a corner while Kodet's findings land as UI cards, one per beat: **Opens dark, looks like an ad** · **No football until 0:17** · **Ends on black, nobody rewatches** | Kodet's voice from the call feature: "Your opening fades in from black. That reads like an ad, so people swipe." |
| 0:16–0:20 | A comparison card against his own Shorts: **This Short 39% stayed** vs **Score Bugs 84% stayed**. | "It compared it to my best Shorts, not to strangers." |
| 0:20–0:25 | Fast montage of three cards: **Plans your next post** · **Drafts your comment replies** · **Waits for your approval** | "Then it plans the next one, drafts my replies, and nothing posts until I say so." |
| 0:25–0:30 | The "Call with Kodet" green bar on a phone. The CTA sits over a bright frame. | "Your analyst, on call. Comment **KODET** for early access." |

**Hook options for A/B testing in the first 2 seconds:**
1. "61% of people swiped away my Short." (default)
2. "YouTube tells you what happened. Kodet tells you why."
3. "My Short flopped. I called my AI and asked why."

## Production notes, borrowing from the Invideo approach

- **Motion graphics:** use Claude for the UI overlay cards, the stamps and the comparison card, the way Invideo used Claude Motion. Give it Kodet's real colors and the Daylight and dark themes as style references.
- **Iterate, don't one-shot.** Invideo said their first one-prompt version "looked like generic slop." Plan 3–5 rounds with style references.
- **Use real footage:** screen recordings of Kodet Insights at phone width, the Chiefs vs Bills Short, and a real call with Kodet for the voice.
- **Music:** YouTube Audio Library, as with Short 06, so there are no rights issues.
